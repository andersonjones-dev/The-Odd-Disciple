Sealed

Design prototype for Impostor mode. Five screens, two directions.

Recommendation: build Direction B, then borrow one thing from A

B is the stronger identity. A looks like a very good version of the game that inspired this brief, and a party game store already holds a dozen of those. B looks like nothing else on the shelf, and every part of it comes from the subject: ink, manuscripts, wax seals, constellations. A youth leader can say what it is in one sentence, and that is the test of an ownable identity.

The mechanics match the metaphor

Breaking a wax seal to read a secret is the actual game action. In A the seal is decoration on a plaque. In B it is the whole reveal, and it comes back at the verdict when the seal cracks open.

It stays calm when the room gets loud

Fewer glows and gradients means text stays readable on a phone passed around a dim room. The magenta is rare, so when it appears it means something: the current speaker, your vote, the impostor.

It scales to the other modes

A Mafia mode and Bible mystery packs can reuse the same folio, ledger and seal pieces with new text, where A would need new hero art for each mode.

Take from A: the energy of the verdict. Let the final reveal flare to full electric magenta for one second before settling back into ink. Watch out for: the antique display face is hard to read below about 20px, so it stays on headings and names, and every instruction uses the sans. A younger group may find B too serious, so keep the copy playful and the motion snappy.
